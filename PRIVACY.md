# Chronicle Keeper Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. This policy covers Chronicle Keeper for Mac, iPad and iPhone.

**Chronicle Keeper collects no data.** It has no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and
no third-party code that reports anything. The only things it sends anywhere but paizo.com are the names of
adventures: what you type into "Who can play...?" and the adventures in your sessions, which go to Warhorn to be
looked up (see "Network").

## Your Paizo account

Chronicle Keeper signs in to paizo.com with the email address and password you give it, to read your characters,
the adventures they have played and your boons. Your email address and password are kept in the Keychain on your
device and go only to Paizo. The sign-in session is kept in memory and ends when the app closes. Paizo learns what
any sign-in and page request tells it, under Paizo's own privacy policy; the developer sees none of it. Every
request names the app and its project page, so Paizo can tell the traffic apart; it carries no email address.

Signing out removes your email address and password from the Keychain. The demo account needs no Paizo account and
contacts no one.

## What stays on your device

- **Your characters and adventures**, read from paizo.com, are saved on your device.
- **Your own notes, tracks, starting levels, character sheet links and portraits** are saved on your device.
- **Your boon PDFs** are saved on your device, or in your iCloud Drive when you use a build with iCloud turned on.
- **Your settings**, such as the view you last used, are saved on your device.

## iCloud

Builds with iCloud turned on keep your own notes, tracks, links, portraits and boon PDFs the same on your Mac, iPad
and iPhone, through your private iCloud storage. The developer cannot see it. Characters and adventures are not
synced: each device reads paizo.com itself.

## Network

Chronicle Keeper contacts paizo.com to sign in and read your account.

Chronicle Keeper looks adventures up in the catalog of published adventures at [Warhorn](https://warhorn.net), a
website for organizing games that the developer of Chronicle Keeper also runs. Two things are sent to Warhorn: the
text you type into "Who can play...?", and, after your account is read, the number of each numbered adventure in your
sessions (such as "6-04"), to find its cover. The addresses of the covers of adventures without a number come with the
app. Each request carries the app's name and project page and, as any request does, your internet address. Nothing
else from your Paizo account goes with it: no email address, player number, character, date or event. The covers are
loaded from Paizo's image servers, which see your internet address, or from Warhorn's; a cover kept anywhere else is
left out. The demo account does none of this.

The app contacts no one else. Links you click open in your browser, under the browser's and that website's own
policies.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send crash reports to the
developer. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy and contain no personal data from the app.

## Children

Chronicle Keeper collects no data from anyone, including children.
