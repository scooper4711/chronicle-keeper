# Character Keeper Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026. This policy covers Character Keeper for Mac, iPad and iPhone.

**Character Keeper collects no data.** It has no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and
no third-party code that reports anything. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer.

## Your Paizo account

Character Keeper signs in to paizo.com with the email address and password you give it, to read your characters,
the adventures they have played and your boons. Your email address and password are kept in the Keychain on your
device and go only to Paizo. The sign-in session is kept in memory and ends when the app closes. Paizo learns what
any sign-in and page request tells it, under Paizo's own privacy policy; the developer sees none of it. Every
request names the app and its project page, so Paizo can tell the traffic apart; it carries no email address.

Signing out removes your email address and password from the Keychain. The demo account needs no Paizo account and
contacts no one.

## What stays on your device

- **Your characters and adventures**, read from paizo.com, are saved on your device.
- **Your own notes, tracks, starting levels, character sheet links and portraits** are saved on your device.
- **Your boon PDFs** are saved on your device, or in your iCloud Drive when you use a build with iCloud turned on.
- **Your settings**, such as the view you last used, are saved on your device.

## iCloud

Builds with iCloud turned on keep your own notes, tracks, links, portraits and boon PDFs the same on your Mac, iPad
and iPhone, through your private iCloud storage. The developer cannot see it. Characters and adventures are not
synced: each device reads paizo.com itself.

## Network

Character Keeper contacts paizo.com (and the Paizo store, for adventure details) to sign in and read your account.
It contacts no one else. Links you click open in your browser, under the browser's and that website's own policies.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send crash reports to the
developer. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy and contain no personal data from the app.

## Children

Character Keeper collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact
